Precisely for this reason, Figure 2 in the Raft paper clearly marks which
state should be persistent; persistent state is written and flushed to
nonvolatile storage every time it's updated. Whatever state has to be
persisted by a server is persisted before the server issues the next RPC
or replies to an ongoing RPC.

Raft can get by with persisting only a subset of its state, namely:
- currentTerm
- votedFor
- log
