3   HashKV Design
HashKV is a persistent KV store that specifically targets
update-intensive workloads. It improves the management
of value storage atop KV separation to achieve high
update performance. It supports standard KV operations:
PUT (i.e., writing a KV pair), GET (i.e., retrieving the
value of a key), DELETE (i.e., deleting a KV pair), and
SCAN (i.e., retrieving the values of a range of keys).
